About

I design and evaluate deep-learning methods for omnidirectional imagery— semantic / referring / open-vocabulary segmentation, crowd counting, and multi-task benchmarks— with lightweight, near-zero-parameter-overhead attention and careful evaluation. Advisor: Prof. Yanyong Zhang (张燕咏). Thesis: Exploring Segmentation in Panoramic Vision Using Attention Mechanisms.
